Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Yana Ellis uses information submitted through the website calculators and contact forms. The information is used only to review project requests, respond to potential clients and prepare a confirmed project quote.

Information Collected

The forms may collect your name, email, selected contact method, messenger or social media handle, project details, calculator answers, inspiration links, budget response, deadline request and other notes you choose to provide.

How It Is Used

  • To review your project request and understand the scope.
  • To contact you using the method you selected.
  • To prepare a manual final quote, timeline and project scope.
  • To prevent spam, abuse or unsafe submissions.

Storage And Sharing

Submitted requests may be delivered through email or Telegram to Yana Ellis. Your details are not sold, published or shared for advertising. Third-party delivery services may process the data only as needed to deliver the request.

Project Materials

You do not need to upload all project materials through the calculator. If files are needed, Yana Ellis will ask for them after reviewing your request.

Your Choices

You can request deletion or correction of submitted information by contacting oh.yanyoellis@gmail.com.
